Data Deletion Instructions – Barber4You

Last updated: November 11, 2026

You can ask us to delete all data our Instagram messaging tool holds about you (your username, Instagram-scoped ID, comments, messages and any details you gave us in the chat).

How to request deletion

  1. Send a direct message to @barber4you.il on Instagram with the word "delete", or
  2. Email GetSocia@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and your Instagram username.

We will delete your data from our systems within 30 days and confirm by reply. Messages that already exist inside Instagram itself are controlled by Instagram; you can delete your own conversation in the Instagram app.

You can also remove our access at any time in Instagram: Settings → Messages → Connected tools, or by blocking @barber4you.il.

See also our Privacy Policy.
